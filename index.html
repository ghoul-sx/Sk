About Sparks

Sparks is a free, anonymous text chat that connects you with a random stranger. There's no sign-up, no login, and nothing is stored once a conversation ends.

It runs without a traditional backend or database — matching and messages travel over a public real-time network, and the site itself is supported by the ads you see on these pages, not by your data.

Privacy Policy

Sparks does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal details.

Chat messages are not stored on any server controlled by Sparks — they are relayed in real time and disappear once a conversation ends. A random, temporary identifier is generated in your browser each visit; it isn't linked to your identity and isn't saved after you close the tab.

If you submit a report, the report content (including a short snippet of the recent conversation) is sent to the site owner for safety review. Your browser also locally remembers a small, anonymous count of safety flags to enforce temporary restrictions — this stays on your device only.

Third parties

Pages on this site display ads from a third-party ad network, which may use cookies in line with their own policies. Messages are relayed through a public message broker that is not operated by Sparks.

Terms of Service

By using Sparks you confirm you are 18 years of age or older.

Sparks is provided as-is, free of charge, with no guarantee of uptime, message delivery, or availability. Conversations are unmoderated in real time; use your own judgment and don't share information you wouldn't want a stranger to have.

Don't use Sparks for spam, harassment, illegal content, or to target or exploit anyone — especially minors. Violating this can result in being reported and restricted from the service.
